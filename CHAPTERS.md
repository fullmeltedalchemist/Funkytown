# Foxglove Ridge story outline

## 1. The Porch Light
You return to Vale House after getting a letter in your mother's handwriting. The porch light has been left on. Her field journal draws a route through town, but a second set of annotations describes places that don't exist anymore. The player follows the real ford to Foxglove Camp, using the hook to recover a missing journal insert from the house eaves. The lodge letter confirms she went through the arch voluntarily. The current game contains the journal, letter, and arch sequence. The eaves insert is planned.

## 2. The Water Runs Uphill
At the widened creek, a flood gauge begins ticking backward. The post office holds an undelivered warning addressed to a family the player knows. Compare it with the survey marks along the pond and open the spillway to expose a buried route marker. When the creek reverses, a familiar voice calls from the opposite bank, repeating the player's own words a second too early.

## 3. The Second Voice
Cross into the Echo Grid and follow the memory toward a distorted Vale House. Grapple through the gaps between the reflected rooflines. Recover the original recording and discover that Mom stayed behind to keep the door shut. The copy only knows events that happened within earshot of the creek. That gives the player a way to test which voice is real.

## 4. The Way Back
Return to town carrying the real recording. Use the journal, the post office warning, and the spillway gauge to open the safe crossing. Decide whether to shut the arch behind Mom or leave it accessible to recover the missing townspeople. A short return through Vale House shows the consequences of the choice.

Chapters 2–4 are design beats for implementation. The current Echo Grid memory is an early version of the reveal in Chapter 3.


## Playable objective pass

- After reading the journal, carry the field receiver to the camp tuning stand and tune to channel 5 to unlock the lodge letter.
- Use the lodge letter to set three sluice valves around the creek and open the Echo Grid arch.
- Start the 42-second Echo Grid trial, hit the three lit receivers in sequence with physical projectiles, then recover the memory. Restart at the receiver if time runs out.

This pass establishes the playable loop. The missing-person investigation, expanded grappling route, and branching Chapter 4 ending are still planned.
